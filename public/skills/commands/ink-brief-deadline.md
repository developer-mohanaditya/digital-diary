Use the `ink` skill. Unit `one`, depth `brief`, category `deadline`: one or two sentences, no markup.

A deadline needs a date. Pass `due:YYYY-MM-DD`, and ask for it rather than filing a reminder
that cannot remind anything.

The subject, when the owner named one, is what they wrote beside the mention.

File it as one request, report the receipt, and stop.
