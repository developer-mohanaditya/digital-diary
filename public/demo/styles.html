INKEntry style ideas - same 6 notes, 4 looks

Four ways to show entries

Same six notes in each. No big cards. Pick the one that feels right on your phone.

1. Bullet log2. Timeline rail3. Grouped ledger4. Reader stream

1. Bullet log

True bullet journal rapid log. The mark says the type: dot thought, dash note, arrow migrated task, star priority. Category is a tiny dot plus label at the end. Tap a line for raw text and Move.
